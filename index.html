Check your bill in 5 steps

  1. Find your reference number — top-left of your paper bill, above the barcode, labelled “Ref No”. It is 14 digits.
  2. Note whether you are Urban (U) or Rural (R) — it is shown next to the reference number on the bill.
  3. Enter the number and pick U or R in the checker box above, then press Open Official Portal.
  4. The official PITC portal opens in a new tab — enter the same reference number and U/R there to view your bill.
  5. View, download or print your bill from the portal. The printed copy is accepted for payment at banks and other payment points.

When will my bill appear online?

Bills are generally generated roughly between the 1st and 10th of the month, and the bill usually appears online 24 to 48 hours after generation. If you check too early, the portal may show no record — wait a day and try again. If it is still not showing after that, see LESCO Bill Not Showing / Record Not Found.

Your LESCO Bill Cycle — Month by Month

LESCO bills run on a monthly cycle. Every month follows the same four steps — the dates are printed on your bill, and your reference number never changes, so the check is the same every month.

StepWhat happensWhat you do
1. Meter readingYour meter is read for the monthNote your own reading — a dated photo helps if the bill reading is wrong
2. Bill issuedYour monthly bill is prepared — generally generated roughly between the 1st and 10th of the monthIf the paper bill has not arrived, check online on the portal — the bill usually appears 24 to 48 hours after generation
3. Due dateThe last date to pay without a surcharge, printed on the billPay before this date — a late paper bill does not move the due date
4. PayPay at a bank, ATM, JazzCash, Easypaisa or post officeKeep your receipt safe — after the due date a 10% late payment surcharge applies

Exact reading and due dates differ by area and month — they are printed on your own bill. This site does not publish fixed dates, because they change every cycle.

QTA — Quarterly Tariff AdjustmentRs 0.5194 per unitS.R.O. 1501 — the quarterly tariff adjustment, applied on September to November 2026 bills

What is FCA? FCA is a monthly fuel-cost adjustment, not a permanent tariff rise. It passes the change in fuel cost through to your bill — so it can go up or down from month to month as fuel prices change. The rate above is for November 2026 bills only; next month's FCA will be different.

How to check it on your bill: look for the “F.P.A.” line on your paper bill. It shows the per-unit FCA rate and the total FCA amount for your units. Your units × the FCA rate = the FCA amount on your bill. If the F.P.A. line is missing or the rate does not match, read your bill line by line in Understanding Your LESCO Bill, or call the helpline on 118.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my LESCO bill online?

Enter your 14-digit reference number and select Urban (U) or Rural (R) in the checker box above, then press Open Official Portal. The official PITC portal (bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill) opens in a new tab, where you enter the same details to view and print your bill. There is no login and no fee.

Where is my LESCO reference number?

At the top-left of your paper bill, above the barcode, labelled “Ref No”. It is 14 digits: 2 for the batch, 5 for the sub-division, and 7 for the reference itself. See where to find it for the full layout.

What does U and R mean?

U stands for Urban and R stands for Rural. Pick the one shown next to your reference number on your bill. Choosing the wrong one can show the wrong record or no record at all.

Can I check my LESCO bill by Customer ID?

Yes. Your 10-digit Customer ID works on both the PITC portal and the LESCO website (lesco.gov.pk, Customer Bill page). Use it if you do not have your reference number handy.

When does my LESCO bill appear online?

Usually 24 to 48 hours after it is generated. Bills are generally generated roughly between the 1st and 10th of the month, so if it is not showing yet, wait a day and check again.

What should I do if my bill is not showing or looks wrong?

First re-check the 14 digits and the U/R selection — one wrong digit gives a “record not found” result. If the details are correct, call the LESCO helpline on 118, or file a complaint through CCMS+ at ccms.pitc.com.pk/complaint. See Bill Not Showing and Wrong Bill Complaint for the full steps.

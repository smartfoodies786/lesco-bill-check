LESCO Reference Number: Where to Find It

Your reference number is the one number you need to check your LESCO bill online. It is printed on every paper bill, and it stays the same for your connection month after month.

Where it is on the bill

Look at the top-left of your bill, above the barcode, where it is labelled "Ref No". That is the position used on LESCO bills. Copy all 14 digits exactly, with no spaces or dashes.

Annotated layout — top of a LESCO bill:

  • Top-left: "Ref No" — your 14-digit reference number, printed above the barcode.
  • Nearby: your Customer ID (10 digits) and your connection details, including whether the connection is Urban or Rural.
  • Elsewhere on the bill: the meter number, the billing month, the due date and the amount payable.

The 2 + 5 + 7 structure

The reference number is 14 digits, made of three parts:

PartDigitsWhat it is
BatchFirst 2 digitsYour billing batch
Sub-divisionNext 5 digitsYour sub-division code
ReferenceLast 7 digitsYour individual reference within that sub-division

Example of the structure (digits shown as placeholders, not a real number): XX XXXXX XXXXXXX — 2 + 5 + 7 = 14 digits. On the LESCO website's Customer Bill page, these three parts go into three separate boxes (Batch No., Sub Division, Reference No.). On the PITC portal, you type all 14 digits together.

U vs R — Urban or Rural

When you check your bill on the PITC portal (bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill), you also choose U or R:

  • U — Urban: connections in urban areas.
  • R — Rural: connections in rural areas.

Pick the one that matches your connection, as shown on your bill. Choosing the wrong one is a common reason for a "record not found" error — see LESCO Bill Not Showing / Record Not Found.

Reference number vs Customer ID vs meter number

NumberDigitsWhere to find itWhat it is used for
Reference number14Top-left of the bill, labelled "Ref No", above the barcodeChecking your bill on both official portals
Customer ID10Printed on the bill near the reference numberAn alternative way to check your bill on the PITC portal — use it if you cannot read the reference number
Meter numberPrinted on your electricity meter and on the billOn the meter itselfIdentifies the physical meter — it is not used to look up your bill on the official portals

Do not type your meter number into the reference-number box: the portal will not find your bill. Use the 14-digit reference number, or the 10-digit Customer ID.

Lost your bill? How to recover the reference number

  1. An old bill: the reference number never changes, so any previous month's bill has the same number. Check an old paper bill, a saved PDF, or a photo of a bill.
  2. Customer ID: if you have the 10-digit Customer ID (from an old bill or a payment receipt), use that on the PITC portal instead — you do not need the reference number.
  3. Payment receipts: JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank payment records often show the reference number or Customer ID used for a past payment.
  4. Call the helpline: if you have no old bill, no Customer ID and no receipt, call the LESCO helpline 118 and ask how to recover your reference number.
  5. Visit the office: your nearest LESCO customer service centre or sub-division office can look up your connection — take your CNIC and proof of the premises (for example, the property documents) with you.

Tip: once you have found your reference number, save it somewhere safe — in your phone's notes, for example — and note whether it is U or R. You will need the same number every month.

Frequently asked questions

Is my reference number the same every month?

Yes. The 14-digit reference number stays the same for your connection. Only the bill amount, units and due date change each month.

Can I check my bill with my meter number?

No. The official portals look bills up by reference number or Customer ID, not by meter number.

What if I type the reference number correctly and still get an error?

Check the U/R selector first — the wrong choice gives an error. Then check for a mistyped digit. If it still fails, see LESCO Bill Not Showing / Record Not Found.

Is it safe to share my reference number?

Your reference number is used to view your bill, so share it only with people you trust and only when needed — for example, with LESCO staff or when paying at a counter. This guide site never asks for it.

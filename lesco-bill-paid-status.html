How to Check If Your LESCO Bill Is Paid

There is no separate “paid” page on the official portal — the portal shows your current bill. The sure way to check whether a bill is paid is your next bill's arrears line together with your payment receipt. Here are the three checks, in order.

Check 1 — the next bill's arrears line

When a new bill is generated, any amount from a previous bill that was not paid appears on it as arrears, and the total payable includes it. So:

  • If you paid the previous bill in full and on time, the next bill should show no arrears for that month.
  • If an old amount appears as arrears on the new bill, your payment was not recorded against it — even if you paid. That is the moment to act (see below).
  • If you paid after the due date, a 10% Late Payment Surcharge may still appear as its own line, even though the bill itself is now paid. The surcharge is for the late payment, not an unpaid bill.

Check your bill on the official portal: How to Check LESCO Bill Online. Tafseel: Understanding Your LESCO Bill (arrears section).

Check 2 — your payment receipt or transaction ID

Every payment leaves a proof. Keep it for 6 months — exactly for this situation:

  • Bank app or wallet (JazzCash, Easypaisa, 1LINK bank app) — the confirmation message and transaction ID from the app.
  • ATM — the printed slip from the machine.
  • Bank counter or post office — the stamped receipt on the bill or the payment slip.
  • Auto-debit — the monthly confirmation message and transaction ID from the app or bank.

If the next bill shows arrears for a month you paid, this receipt is your proof. Without it, proving the payment is much harder.

Check 3 — your bank or wallet app history

Open the app you paid with and look at its payment or transaction history. It shows the payment, the date, the amount and the transaction ID. If the payment appears there, the money left your account — the question is only whether LESCO recorded it, which the next bill's arrears line answers.

Paid, but the bill still shows unpaid — what to do

  1. Do not pay the same bill again yet. First confirm with your receipt and the next bill's arrears line.
  2. Keep the receipt ready — transaction ID, date, amount and the channel you paid through.
  3. Contact LESCO helpline 118 with the receipt details, or visit your SDO / Customer Service Centre with the receipt. The office can check the payment against your reference number and correct the record.
  4. If you lost the receipt, check your bank or wallet app history for the transaction — the bank can also give you a statement showing the payment.

The portal does not show a “paid” tick or a payment history. It shows the current bill. Any third-party website or app that claims to show your LESCO payment status is not official — do not type your reference number or personal details into it. Use only the official portal and your own receipt.

Frequently asked questions — LESCO Bill Paid Status

I paid yesterday — why does the bill on the portal still show the amount?

The bill on the portal is the bill that was generated for the month. A payment does not change the generated bill — it shows up on the next bill, where the arrears line should be clear. Check the next bill, not the same day's portal view.

What proof of payment should I keep?

The receipt or transaction ID, the date, the amount and the payment channel. Keep receipts for 6 months — that is exactly how long you may need them if a paid bill shows as arrears.

I lost my payment receipt — what now?

Check your bank or wallet app's payment history for the transaction ID and date. The bank can also give you a statement showing the payment. Take that to the LESCO office if the bill still shows arrears.

I paid twice by mistake — what should I do?

Do not assume how it will be adjusted. Contact helpline 118 or your SDO office with both receipts and ask how the extra payment is treated on your account.

Does the portal show whether my bill is paid?

No. The portal shows your current bill. The arrears line on the next bill and your own receipt are the two real checks.

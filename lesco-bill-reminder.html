LESCO Bill Reminder

Every month the same story: the paper bill arrives late, or never arrives, the due date passes, and a 10% late payment surcharge appears on the next bill. A late paper bill does not move the due date. The fix is simple — set a reminder once, and it repeats every month.

This site sends no alerts. This site never sends you SMS, email or app alerts, and it never stores your reference number, phone number or any other detail. The reminder lives on your own phone — set it up below, once, and it repeats every month.

Set a monthly reminder on your phone

Use the calendar app already on your phone — no new app needed:

  1. Open your phone's calendar app (Google Calendar, Samsung Calendar, Apple Calendar — any of them works).
  2. Create a new event, repeating monthly — set it for around the 5th of each month and name it “Check LESCO bill”. LESCO bills are generally generated roughly between the 1st and 10th of the month, and the bill usually appears online 24 to 48 hours after generation — so checking in the first week catches it.
  3. Add a second reminder — set another monthly event a few days before the due date printed on your bill, named “Pay LESCO bill”. The due date is printed on every bill and it changes each month, so check the date on the latest bill when you set this one.
  4. When the reminder rings, check your bill on the official portal: LESCO Bill Check Online. Pay before the due date and keep your receipt.

If your bill is not showing when the reminder rings, wait a day and check again — see Bill Not Showing.

Auto-debit — the automatic option

If you do not want to remember at all, set up auto-debit once and the bill is paid automatically each month:

  • Most banks and mobile wallets let you set up an automatic payment (auto-debit) for a utility bill. Set it up with your 14-digit LESCO reference number in your bank app or wallet.
  • After you set it up: you should get a confirmation message and transaction ID from the app or bank. Check your next bill — the payment should appear and the previous amount should not be shown as arrears.
  • Keep your account funded — if there is not enough balance when the payment is due, the payment fails and the surcharge still applies.
  • If the payment does not appear on your bill, keep the receipt and contact the helpline on 118 or visit a LESCO office with the receipt.

What happens if you miss the due date?

  • 10% late payment surcharge is added to the bill after the due date.
  • The surcharge does not go away by itself — it appears as arrears on the next bill, so pay the full amount, including the surcharge, before the next due date.
  • A late paper bill is not an excuse — the due date is fixed when the bill is generated. Check your bill online if the paper bill has not arrived.
  • Pay as soon as you realise — the longer you wait, the more the arrears grow on the next bill.

Frequently asked questions — LESCO Bill Reminder

Does this site send me bill reminders or alerts?

No. This site sends no SMS, email or app alerts, and it stores nothing — not your reference number, not your phone number. The reminder is set on your own phone's calendar, as explained above. Bijli Baji, the chatbot on this site, also sends no reminders.

How do I set a monthly reminder on my phone?

Open your phone's calendar app, create a new event that repeats monthly, set it for around the 5th of each month and name it “Check LESCO bill”. Add a second monthly reminder a few days before the due date printed on your bill, named “Pay LESCO bill”.

What is auto-debit and is it safe?

Auto-debit is an automatic payment you set up once in your bank app or mobile wallet with your LESCO reference number — the bill is then paid automatically each month. You get a confirmation message and transaction ID for each payment. Keep enough balance in the account, and check the first bill after setup to confirm the payment appeared.

I missed the due date — what should I do now?

Pay the bill as soon as possible, including the 10% late payment surcharge. The surcharge appears as arrears on your next bill. If the paper bill arrived late, check your bill online next month instead of waiting for the paper copy.

My bill has not arrived yet — how do I know the due date?

Check your bill online on the official PITC portal — the bill usually appears 24 to 48 hours after generation, and the due date is printed on it. See How to Check LESCO Bill Online.

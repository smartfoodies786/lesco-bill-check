LESCO Bill Name Change — How to Change the Name on Your Bill

Changing the name on a LESCO bill is an office-based process. The official portal has no name-change option — you cannot change the name online. You apply in person at your sub-division's SDO office or Customer Service Centre (CSC).

Where to go

Visit the SDO office or Customer Service Centre of the sub-division your connection belongs to — the same office that serves your area. If you are not sure which sub-division you are in, your bill shows it: the reference number's 5-digit sub-division part identifies it, and the helpline 118 can guide you to the right office.

What to take with you

Take your original CNIC and a document that proves the property or the connection is yours — for example, a property document or a previous bill in your name. Take a copy of a recent bill as well, so the office can find your connection quickly by its reference number.

This page does not publish a document list or a fee. The exact list of documents — and whether any fee applies — is not verified from an official source, so it is not printed here. Confirm the exact list at your SDO office before you go, so you do not make two trips. Any website that prints a “complete document list” or a “name-change fee” for LESCO without naming its source is guessing.

When do you need a name change?

  • You bought a property — the bill still shows the previous owner's name.
  • Inheritance — the connection passes to a family member after the owner's death.
  • Spelling correction — your name is spelled wrong on the bill.
  • Ownership transfer — the property changed hands and the new owner wants the bill in their own name.

What happens after you apply?

  1. The office takes your application and documents and gives you a receipt or reference for it — keep that receipt.
  2. The changed name appears on a later bill, once the office processes the change. It does not change on the bill you already have.
  3. Keep paying the bill in the meantime — a name change does not pause billing, and the reference number stays the same.
  4. Keep your old bills and receipts until the new name appears on the bill. If the name has not changed after a reasonable time, follow up at the same office with your application receipt.

Frequently asked questions — LESCO Bill Name Change

Can I change the name on my LESCO bill online?

No. The official portal has no name-change option. A name change is an office-based process at your SDO office or Customer Service Centre.

What documents are needed for a name change?

Take your original CNIC and proof that the property or connection is yours, such as a property document or a previous bill in your name. Confirm the exact list at your SDO office before you go — this page does not print a list, because the exact requirement is not verified.

Is there a fee for a name change?

Not published here — whether any fee applies is not verified from an official source. Ask at the SDO office when you apply.

How long does a name change take?

No fixed time is published. The changed name appears on a later bill once the office processes it. If it has not appeared after a reasonable time, follow up at the same office with your application receipt.

Does the reference number change with the name?

No. The reference number belongs to the connection, not the person. Only the name on the bill changes; the reference number stays the same, so your bill-check and payment routine does not change.

LESCO Protected vs Unprotected Consumers

Whether you are a "protected" or "unprotected" consumer is one of the biggest factors in your LESCO bill. Protected consumers pay lower per-unit rates; unprotected consumers pay higher rates. The difference is decided entirely by how many units you used in the previous months.

The protected rule

You are a protected consumer if you used 200 units or less per month for the previous 6 consecutive months. All six months must be at or below 200 units — if even one of those six months goes above 200 units, you lose protected status.

In short: 6 months in a row at 200 units or less = protected. Any month above 200 in that 6-month window = unprotected.

No rates on this page — deliberately. The exact per-unit rates for protected and unprotected slabs change when NEPRA issues a new notification, and the analysis behind this guide gives no NEPRA notification date and no dated rates for LESCO. Other websites publish protected/unprotected rates with no date and no NEPRA citation — those figures cannot be trusted as current. To find the rates that apply to your bill right now, read the rates printed on your current bill and check the current NEPRA notification via lesco.gov.pk.

Monthly re-check — status can change every month

Protected status is not permanent. It is re-checked every month, looking back at the previous 6 months:

  • You can lose it in one month. One hot month with the air conditioner running — a single month above 200 units — moves you to unprotected, and your per-unit rates go up from the next bill.
  • You can get it back. After 6 consecutive months back at 200 units or less, you become protected again.
  • That is why a bill can jump suddenly. If your units were, say, 190 a month for five months and then 250 in the sixth, the bill does not just rise because of the extra 60 units — the higher unprotected rates apply, so the increase is larger than the extra units alone would suggest.

How to stay protected (if you can)

  • Watch your monthly units on each bill — the units used are printed on the bill and shown on the official portals.
  • If you are close to 200 units in a month, cutting back on heavy appliances (air conditioner, water heater, iron, washing machine use) before the meter-reading date can keep you under the line.
  • Remember that it is the previous 6 months that count, not just this month — one high month affects the next bills until it drops out of the 6-month window.

Tip: if your bill suddenly jumps, check two things first: your units this month, and whether you were protected last month and unprotected this month. That change alone often explains the jump — see Understanding Your LESCO Bill.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if I am protected or unprotected?

Your bill shows your consumer category. You can also work it out yourself: if you used 200 units or less in each of the previous 6 months, you are protected.

I used less than 200 units this month, but my bill is still high. Why?

Because protected status depends on the previous 6 months, not just this month. If any of the last 6 months was above 200 units, you are unprotected this month even though this month's usage was low.

How long does it take to become protected again?

Six consecutive months at 200 units or less, after which the re-check puts you back in the protected category.

Where can I find the current protected and unprotected rates?

On your current bill, and in the current NEPRA notification — check lesco.gov.pk for the latest notification. This guide does not publish rates, because rates without a notification date go out of date.

How to Pay LESCO Bill Online

Once you have checked your bill, you can pay it online in a few minutes — or in person if you prefer. All of the methods below accept your 14-digit LESCO reference number. Pay on or before the due date printed on the bill to avoid the late payment surcharge.

Late payment surcharge: if you pay after the due date, a Late Payment Surcharge (LPS) of 10% applies. Paying even one day late can add 10% to the bill, so check the due date first — it is printed on the bill and shown on the official portals.

Pay online

JazzCash

  1. Open the JazzCash app and go to bill payments / utility bills.
  2. Select electricity, then LESCO.
  3. Enter your 14-digit reference number and confirm the amount and due date match your bill.
  4. Pay and save the confirmation message and transaction ID.

Easypaisa

  1. Open the Easypaisa app and go to bill payments / utility bills.
  2. Select electricity, then LESCO.
  3. Enter your 14-digit reference number and confirm the amount and due date match your bill.
  4. Pay and save the confirmation message and transaction ID.

Any 1LINK bank app

  1. Open your bank's mobile app (any bank on the 1LINK network) and go to bill payments.
  2. Select electricity / LESCO and enter your 14-digit reference number.
  3. Confirm the amount and due date match your bill, then pay.
  4. Save the transaction receipt or screenshot.

Before paying through any app, always check that the name, amount and due date shown match your bill. If they do not match, stop and re-check the reference number.

Keep your receipt for 6 months

Keep every payment receipt — the app confirmation, the ATM slip, or the stamped counter receipt — for at least 6 months. If a payment is ever shown as unpaid, or if an old amount appears as arrears on a later bill, the receipt is your proof that you paid. Save app confirmations as screenshots as well as keeping the SMS.

Auto-debit — pay without remembering each month

Most banks and mobile wallets let you set up an automatic payment (auto-debit) for a utility bill. Once set up with your LESCO reference number, the bill is paid automatically each month from your account or wallet. If you set this up:

  • Make sure there is enough balance in the account before the due date each month.
  • Check the first two automatic payments yourself, to confirm the right amount was taken on time.
  • Remember the 10% late surcharge still applies if the auto-debit fails — for example, if the balance is too low — so check the payment went through.

Tip: paying a day or two before the due date is safer than paying on the last day — if an app or ATM has a problem on the due date, you still have time to pay another way.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I pay after the due date?

A Late Payment Surcharge of 10% applies. The surcharge is added to the amount you owe.

Can I pay a LESCO bill without the paper bill?

Yes. You only need the 14-digit reference number (or Customer ID, depending on the payment channel). Check your bill online first to confirm the amount and due date — see How to Check LESCO Bill Online.

How do I know my online payment went through?

You should get a confirmation message and transaction ID from the app or bank. Check your next bill: the payment should appear and the previous amount should not be shown as arrears. If it is, keep the receipt and contact the helpline 118 or visit a LESCO office with the receipt.

Is there any extra charge for paying online?

This guide does not state a payment-channel fee — any fee would be shown by the app or bank before you confirm the payment. Check the confirmation screen before paying.

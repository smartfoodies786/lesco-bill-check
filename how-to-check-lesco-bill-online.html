How to Check LESCO Bill Online

LESCO serves Lahore, Okara, Sheikhupura, Nankana Sahib and Kasur — around 5 million consumers. You can check your bill online for free, with no login, on either of the two official routes below. This site is a guide only: it does not fetch your bill and never asks you to enter your reference number here. You enter it only on the official portal.

Before you start: keep your 14-digit reference number ready (2 digits batch + 5 digits sub-division + 7 digits reference), and know whether your connection is Urban (U) or Rural (R). Bills are generated roughly between the 1st and 10th of the month, and a new bill appears on the portal 24–48 hours after generation — so if it is early in the month, check again after a day or two.

Route 1: Check on the PITC portal

  1. Open bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill.
  2. Enter your 14-digit reference number (no spaces or dashes), or your 10-digit Customer ID.
  3. Select U for Urban or R for Rural, matching your connection.
  4. Submit. Your current bill appears on screen — amount, due date, units used and the full bill.

Route 2: Check on lesco.gov.pk

  1. Open lesco.gov.pk and go to the "Customer Bill" page.
  2. Enter the Batch No., Sub Division and Reference No. in the three boxes, exactly as printed on your bill.
  3. Submit to view your bill.

When to use which route

  • Use PITC if you only have the reference number or the Customer ID, or if you want the quickest check.
  • Use lesco.gov.pk if you are already on the LESCO website, or if the PITC page is not loading and you want to try the other official route.
  • If neither shows your bill and it is early in the month, wait 24–48 hours after the usual generation window (roughly the 1st–10th). If it still does not show, see LESCO Bill Not Showing / Record Not Found.

Tip: SMS short code 8118 exists — confirm the format on lesco.gov.pk before using it, as published formats differ.

How to download and print your bill as PDF

  1. Open your bill on either official portal above.
  2. Use your browser's print option (usually Ctrl+P on a computer, or Share → Print on a phone).
  3. Choose "Save as PDF" as the printer/destination to download a PDF copy, or choose your printer to print a paper copy.
  4. Save the PDF with the month in the file name (for example, "LESCO-bill-Nov-2026.pdf") so you can find it later.

A printed or PDF copy of the bill from the official portal is a duplicate bill. It shows the same reference number, amount and due date as the paper bill, so it can be used for payment at a bank counter or post office just like the original.

What this site does not do

This site never fetches your bill, never asks for your reference number, CNIC or meter number, and never stores any number you type. Any website that asks for payment or personal details to "check" your LESCO bill is not the official portal — use only the two official routes above.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account to check my LESCO bill?

No. Both official routes work without a login, and there is no fee.

Can I check my bill with my Customer ID instead of the reference number?

Yes. The 10-digit Customer ID works on the PITC portal. See LESCO Reference Number: Where to Find It.

How soon after the bill is generated can I see it online?

Usually 24–48 hours after generation. Bills are generated roughly between the 1st and 10th of the month.

Can I check a bill from a previous month?

The portals show the current bill. This guide does not claim a bill-history feature — check the portal itself for what it shows for your connection.

Where do I complain if the bill amount is wrong?

See LESCO Wrong Bill / Overbilling Complaint, or call the LESCO helpline 118.

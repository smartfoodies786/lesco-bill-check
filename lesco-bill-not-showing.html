LESCO Bill Not Showing / Record Not Found

You typed your reference number into the official portal and got nothing back — or an error. Work through the five causes below in order. They are listed from most likely to least likely, and most cases are fixed by one of the first three.

The exact PITC error wording: when the portal cannot find the number you entered, it shows "Given Ref/App No is invalid". That message means the portal did not recognise the number (or the U/R choice) you entered — it does not necessarily mean your connection does not exist.

The 5 causes, in likelihood order

1. The bill has not been generated yet (most likely, early in the month)

Why it happens: LESCO bills are generated roughly between the 1st and 10th of the month, and a new bill appears on the portal 24–48 hours after generation. If you check before your bill is generated, there is no record to show yet.

Fix: wait and check again after a day or two. If it is still early in the month (before the 10th), this is almost certainly the cause. Check last month's bill for your usual generation pattern.

2. A mistyped reference number

Why it happens: one wrong digit, a missing digit, or a space or dash typed into the 14-digit number is enough for the portal to return "Given Ref/App No is invalid".

Fix: re-read the number from your paper bill — top-left, above the barcode, labelled "Ref No" — and type all 14 digits with no spaces or dashes. Count the digits as you type: 2 + 5 + 7 = 14. Try your 10-digit Customer ID on the PITC portal instead, as a cross-check.

3. Wrong U / R selection

Why it happens: on the PITC portal you choose U (Urban) or R (Rural) alongside the reference number. If the choice does not match your connection, the portal will not find the record.

Fix: check whether your connection is Urban or Rural as shown on your bill, and select the matching option. If you are unsure, try the other option — one of the two will match.

4. Wrong batch or sub-division on the lesco.gov.pk route

Why it happens: the LESCO website's Customer Bill page asks for Batch No., Sub Division and Reference No. in three separate boxes. If the batch or sub-division is entered wrongly — or the three parts are mixed up — the lookup fails, even if the reference number itself is right.

Fix: re-read the three parts from your bill and enter each in its own box. Then cross-check on the PITC portal with the full 14-digit number (Route 1), which does not need the batch and sub-division separately. See How to Check LESCO Bill Online and LESCO Reference Number: Where to Find It.

5. New connection, or a portal problem (least likely)

Why it happens: a brand-new connection may not be in the online system yet, or the portal itself may be temporarily down or slow.

Fix: try again later in the day, and try the other official route (if PITC fails, try lesco.gov.pk, and vice versa). For a new connection, allow time for the first bill to be generated — if the first bill still does not appear after the usual generation window, call the helpline (below).

Tip: SMS short code 8118 exists — confirm the format on lesco.gov.pk before using it, as published formats differ.

When to call 118

Call the LESCO helpline 118 if:

  • You have checked all five causes above — the number is correct, the U/R choice matches, and it is past the usual generation window (after the 10th, plus 48 hours) — and the bill still does not show.
  • The bill showed last month with the same reference number but does not show this month.
  • You have a new connection and no bill has appeared after the first generation window.
  • The portal shows an error other than "Given Ref/App No is invalid" that you cannot resolve by retrying.

When you call, have your 14-digit reference number (and your Customer ID, if you have it) ready.

Frequently asked questions

What does "Given Ref/App No is invalid" mean?

It is the PITC portal's exact error message when it cannot find the reference number you entered. It usually means a typing mistake, the wrong U/R choice, or that the bill has not been generated yet — not that your connection does not exist.

I checked on the 5th of the month and my bill is not showing. Is something wrong?

Probably not. Bills are generated roughly between the 1st and 10th, and appear 24–48 hours after generation. Check again after a couple of days.

Can I check my bill with my meter number if the reference number does not work?

No. The portals look bills up by reference number or Customer ID, not by meter number. Use the 10-digit Customer ID as the alternative to the reference number.

Both portals fail. What now?

Try again later, then call the helpline 118 with your reference number ready — especially if it is past the 10th of the month and the bill still does not show.

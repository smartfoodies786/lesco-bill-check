LESCO Bill Online Check Sheikhupura

LESCO supplies electricity in Sheikhupura district, Punjab. Checking your bill online works the same way in every LESCO district — there is no separate Sheikhupura portal. Use your 14-digit reference number (with U/R) or your 10-digit Customer ID on the official PITC portal.

Official bill page: bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill — “LESCO Web Bill”. Enter your 14-digit reference number, choose U (Urban) or R (Rural) as printed on your bill, and press submit. No login, no fee, no CNIC needed. This site never fetches or stores your number.

How to check your LESCO bill in Sheikhupura — 4 steps

  1. Find your 14-digit reference number — top-left of any old or new bill, above the barcode, labelled “Ref No”. Any old bill works; the number never changes.
  2. Check the U/R letter next to it: U means Urban, R means Rural. Pick the same one on the portal — the wrong letter can show “no record”.
  3. Open the official portal — bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill — and enter the same details. You can also use the LESCO website, lesco.gov.pk (Customer Bill page), with the batch, sub-division and reference number in three boxes.
  4. View, download or print your bill. The printout or PDF is your duplicate bill and can be paid at any bank.

No reference number? See how to find your bill without a reference number. Full detail: reference number guide.

Quick answers for Sheikhupura

  • Helpline: 118 (national electricity helpline). For a formal complaint, use CCMS+ at ccms.pitc.com.pk/complaint.
  • Pay the bill: JazzCash, Easypaisa, any 1LINK bank app, ATM, bank counter or post office. Pay before the due date — late payment adds a 10% surcharge. See payment guide.
  • Wrong bill or complaint: call 118 or file a complaint through CCMS+. See wrong bill complaint.
  • Bill not showing: bills are generated roughly between the 1st and 10th of the month and appear online 24–48 hours after generation. See bill not showing.
  • Duplicate bill: print or save-as-PDF from the official portal — it is the same bill and valid at banks.
  • SMS: SMS short code 8118 exists — confirm the format on lesco.gov.pk before using it, as published formats differ.

Frequently asked questions — Sheikhupura

Can I check my LESCO bill online in Sheikhupura?

Yes. Use the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill with your 14-digit reference number (U/R) or 10-digit Customer ID. The process is the same in every LESCO district, including Sheikhupura.

What is the LESCO helpline for Sheikhupura?

Call the national electricity helpline 118. There is no separate district helpline number — 118 covers all 5 LESCO districts.

Can I check my LESCO bill by Customer ID in Sheikhupura?

Yes. Your 10-digit Customer ID works on the PITC portal and on lesco.gov.pk (Customer Bill page) instead of the reference number.

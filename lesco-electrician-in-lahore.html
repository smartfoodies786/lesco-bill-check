Electrician in Lahore

Many people who come to check their LESCO bill in Lahore also need an electrician — a meter problem, faulty wiring, a tripping breaker, or a load fault. Before you call anyone, first check whose work it is: some jobs belong to LESCO, and some belong to a private electrician. Calling the wrong one wastes your time and money.

Meter ka kaam kabhi khud na karein. Meter, meter ki seal, aur meter tak ki line LESCO ki zimmedari hoti hai. Meter ki seal todein ya meter kholein to detection bill ya legal action ho sakta hai. Meter se mutaliq har masle ke liye LESCO helpline 118 par call karein.

Pehle yeh check karein — kaam LESCO ka hai ya electrician ka?

Aam tor par kaam is tarah banta hai:

Electrician bulane se pehle — 4 cheezein check karein

  1. Main breaker / MCB check karein — aksar bijli sirf tripped breaker ki wajah se jati hai. Breaker ko aik dafa off karke dobara on karein; agar foran phir trip ho jaye to electrician bulayein.
  2. Poori gali check karein — agar parosiyon ki bijli bhi gayi hui hai to supply ka masla hai; electrician nahi, LESCO helpline 118 par call karein.
  3. Bill par reading aur meter milaan karein — agar bill ki reading meter se match nahi karti to masla meter reading ka ho sakta hai; dated photo lein aur 118 par call karein. Tafseel: Wrong Bill Complaint.
  4. Jalne ki boo ya chingari — foran main breaker off karein aur electrician ko bulayein. Khud taar ko haath na lagayein.

Safety tips — kaam shuru karne se pehle

  • Meter ki seal kabhi na todein aur meter kabhi na kholein — yeh LESCO ka kaam hai.
  • Kisi bhi wiring ka kaam shuru karne se pehle main breaker off karein.
  • Live taaron par kaam na karein aur geeli jagah par bijli ka kaam na karwayein.
  • Jalne ki boo, garam socket ya baar-baar trip hone par kaam foran rok dein aur electrician bulayein.
  • Bachon ko khule board, socket ya taaron ke qareeb na jaane dein.
  • Kaam khatam hone par electrician se check karwayein ke sab switches aur breaker theek kaam kar rahe hain.

Electrician kaise dhoondein?

Lahore mein electrician dhoondne ke tareeqe:

  • Parosi ya mohalle ki dukaan se poochein — aik jana-pehchana local electrician aam tor par sab se bharosemand hota hai.
  • Experience poochein — usi kaam ka experience poochein jo aapko karwana hai (wiring, breaker, load fault).
  • Kaam aur charges pehle tay kar lein — kaam shuru karne se pehle ye tay karein ke kya kaam hoga aur charges kya honge. Is page par koi fixed price nahi diya gaya, kyunki charges kaam par depend karte hain.
  • Receipt lein — payment ki receipt ya likha hua hisaab lein, taake baad mein koi ikhtilaf na ho.
  • Meter wala kaam ho to ruk jayein — agar kaam ke doran pata chale ke masla meter ka hai, to kaam rok dein aur 118 par call karein.

Electrician listings — jald aa rahi hain. Is page par abhi kisi electrician ka naam, phone number, price ya review nahi diya gaya. Listings tayyar hote hi yahan shaya ki jayengi — kuch bhi invent karke nahi likha gaya.

Frequently asked questions — Electrician in Lahore

Meter kharab hai — electrician bulana chahiye ya LESCO ko call karein?

LESCO ko — helpline 118 par call karein. Meter, meter ki seal aur meter tak ki line LESCO ki zimmedari hoti hai; private electrician meter ka kaam nahi karta.

Ghar ki wiring ke liye kaun zimmedar hai?

Meter ke baad ghar ki andar ki wiring, switches aur sockets aapki zimmedari hoti hain — inke liye private electrician bulana parta hai.

Poori gali ki bijli gayi hai — electrician bulana chahiye?

Nahi. Poori gali ya area ki bijli jana supply ka masla hota hai — LESCO helpline 118 par call karein, electrician is mein kuch nahi kar sakta.

Meter ki seal tooti hui mile to kya karein?

Seal ko haath na lagayein aur meter na kholein. Foran LESCO helpline 118 par report karein — seal todna detection bill ya legal action ki wajah ban sakta hai.

Electrician ki fees kitni hoti hai?

Is page par koi fixed fees nahi di gayi — charges kaam ki noiyat aur waqt par depend karte hain. Kaam shuru karne se pehle charges tay kar lein aur receipt lein.

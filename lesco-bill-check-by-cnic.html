LESCO Bill Check by CNIC

Plain answer: there is no official CNIC search for LESCO bills. You cannot check your LESCO bill by entering your CNIC number anywhere online. Any website or app that claims you can is not official — do not trust it, and do not type your CNIC into it.

What the official portal actually asks for

The official LESCO Web Bill page (bill.pitc.com.pk/lescobill) asks only for one of these two numbers — no login, no OTP, no CNIC field:

  • 14-digit reference number — 2 digits batch + 5 digits sub-division + 7 digits reference, plus U (Urban) or R (Rural).
  • 10-digit Customer ID — an alternative to the reference number, found on any old bill.

That is all. If a page asks for your CNIC to “check” your bill, it is not the official portal. Tafseel: LESCO Reference Number: Where to Find It.

Find your number instead — the fastest routes

  1. Any old bill or receipt — the reference number is on the top-left of every paper bill, and the Customer ID is on the bill too. A photo of an old bill from a family member works just as well.
  2. A saved PDF — if you saved last month's bill as a PDF, the number is on it. See LESCO Previous Bill.
  3. Call the helpline 118 — explain that you have lost your reference number. Have your CNIC, your name and your address ready. The helpline can guide you on how your connection can be traced.
  4. Visit your Customer Service Centre (CSC) or SDO office with your original CNIC and a document that proves the property or connection is yours. The office staff can look up your connection by CNIC and give you your reference number — and a duplicate bill, free, the same day.

Full recovery steps, in order: LESCO Bill Without Reference Number.

Never share your CNIC photo with a random website, agent or WhatsApp number claiming to check your bill by CNIC. There is no official CNIC bill-check service online. Such sites and apps may misuse your CNIC details. Use only the official portal, the helpline 118, or a LESCO office in person.

Frequently asked questions — LESCO Bill Check by CNIC

Can I check my LESCO bill with my CNIC number online?

No. There is no official CNIC search for LESCO bills. The portal needs your 14-digit reference number or your 10-digit Customer ID.

Why do some websites claim CNIC lookup works?

They are not official. Some third-party sites and apps use “bill check by CNIC” as a headline to collect your details. The official portal has no CNIC field at all. Do not type your CNIC into them.

Can the LESCO office find my bill by CNIC?

Yes — in person. Visit your CSC or SDO office with your original CNIC and a property document. The office staff can look up your connection by CNIC and give you your reference number and a duplicate bill, free, the same day.

I have lost my reference number — what is the fastest route?

First, an old bill or a family member's photo of one — the number is on it. Failing that, call helpline 118 with your CNIC, name and address ready. The sure route is the office visit with your CNIC.

Is my Customer ID the same as my CNIC number?

No. The Customer ID is a 10-digit number printed on your bill — it is not your CNIC. It works on the portal as an alternative to the 14-digit reference number.
